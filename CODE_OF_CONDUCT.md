# Code of conduct

The Void is used by researchers with very different backgrounds, from
students opening their first CIF to people who write simulation codes.
Everyone who takes part here, in issues, discussions or anywhere else
connected to this project, is expected to make that easy.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the
  software or to the field.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree about methods and results
  with evidence.
- Give credit: cite the software, the methods and the data you use, and
  acknowledge other people's contributions.
- Respect other people's data. Do not post structures, results or
  personal information you do not have the right to share.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their
  permission.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here concerns you, contact the maintainer,
Benyamin M. Garmejani, through his GitHub profile
([@benjen-mg](https://github.com/benjen-mg)). Reports are handled
confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block
people who do not follow this code.
